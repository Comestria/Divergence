To get GodStat working, you have to do the following:

GodStat:
Mouth 7X 4Y
Eye 11X 1Y
State: CloseEye
Mug Exceed: 1
Tile 1 10X 1Y
Tile 2 10X 3Y

To get God3Mugs working, you have to do the following:

Mug1 needs to be loaded closest to the center,
Mug 3 furthest from it, and 2 in the middle

God3Mugs1:
Mouth 3X 4Y
Eye 3X 2Y
State: Normal
Mug Exceed: 1
Tile 1 0X 0Y
Tile 2 0X 2Y

God3Mugs2:
Mouth 255X 254Y
Eye 1X 0Y
State: Close Eye
Mug Exceed: 1
Tile 1 3X 254Y
Tile 2 0X 254Y

God3Mugs3
Mouth 0X 0Y
Eye 4X 3Y
State: Normal
Mug Exceed: 1
Tile 1: 4X 254Y
Tile 2: 6X 254Y
